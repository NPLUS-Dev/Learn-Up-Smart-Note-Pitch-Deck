02 Future of Education

How education will change

Once AI takes over the teaching, the roles of teachers and schools shift with it.

🤖
AI
Assistant → Lecturer

AI takes over explaining, setting problems, and grading — so every student can be taught with a curriculum of their own.

👩‍🏫
Teacher
Lecturer → Facilitator

Instead of repeating the same lesson to 30 students, teachers move to finding the students who are stuck, working with them, and keeping them motivated.

🏫
School
Knowledge → Living together

Personalized AI delivers knowledge better. Schools take on what can only be learned between people — collaboration, debate, and attitude.

🎓 This isn't our vision. It's the future that's already coming.
